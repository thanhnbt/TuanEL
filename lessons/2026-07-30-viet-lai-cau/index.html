I Câu điều kiện (Conditional Sentences)

Công thức — If loại 2 (không có thật ở hiện tại)
Gốc: S + don't/can't + V, so S + don't/can't + V
→ If + S + Ved/2, S + would/could + V
Ví dụ 1
I don't have enough money, so I can't buy that dictionary.
⟶
If I had enough money, I could buy that dictionary.
don't have → had · can't buy → could buy
Ví dụ 2
I don't know her phone number, so I don't call her.
⟶
If I knew her phone number, I would call her.
don't know → knew · don't call → would call
Ví dụ 3
He is very lazy, so he gets bad marks.
⟶
If he weren't so lazy, he wouldn't get bad marks.
is → weren't · gets → wouldn't get · "be" luôn dùng "were" trong If loại 2
Công thức — Mệnh lệnh → If loại 1
Gốc: V + ... or + S + will + V
→ If + S + don't/doesn't + V, S + will + V
Ví dụ 1
Work hard or you will fail the final exam.
⟶
If you don't work hard, you will fail the final exam.
"V + or" → "If + don't V" — giữ nguyên vế sau
Ví dụ 2
Stop talking or the teacher will punish you.
⟶
If you don't stop talking, the teacher will punish you.
Thêm chủ ngữ "you" + "don't" trước động từ
Phân biệt: Câu gốc có "don't/can't" → If loại 2 (giả định, lùi thì). Câu gốc có "V + or" → If loại 1 (cảnh báo, giữ nguyên thì).

II Câu gián tiếp (Reported Speech)

Công thức 1 — Câu trần thuật
"S + V ..." → S + said (that) + S + V lùi thì
Ví dụ 1
"I am doing my homework now," said my sister.
⟶
My sister said that she was doing her homework then.
I → she · am doing → was doing · my → her · now → then
Ví dụ 2
"I will visit my grandparents tomorrow," she said.
⟶
She said that she would visit her grandparents the next day.
I → she · will → would · my → her · tomorrow → the next day
Công thức 2 — Câu hỏi Wh-
"Wh- + aux + S + V?" → S + asked + Wh- + S + V lùi thì (không đảo ngữ)
Ví dụ
"Where are you going for your holiday?" she asked me.
⟶
She asked me where I was going for my holiday.
are you going → I was going · your → my · Bỏ dấu "?" — dùng trật tự S + V
Công thức 3 — Câu hỏi Yes/No
"Do/Can + S + V?" → S + asked + if/whether + S + V lùi thì
Ví dụ 1
"Can I borrow your pen?" Nam asked Lan.
⟶
Nam asked Lan if he could borrow her pen.
Can → could · I → he · your → her · thêm if/whether
Ví dụ 2
"Do you like listening to pop music?" he asked her.
⟶
He asked her if she liked listening to pop music.
Do you like → she liked · you → she · thêm if/whether
Công thức 4 — Câu mệnh lệnh
"(Don't) V ...!" → S + told + O + (not) to V
Ví dụ
"Don't play football in the street," the policeman said to the boys.
⟶
The policeman told the boys not to play football in the street.
Don't V → told + O + not to V · "said to" → "told"

III Câu so sánh (Comparatives)

Công thức 1 — So sánh hơn ↔ Không ... bằng
A + V + more ... than B
↔ B + doesn't V as ... as A
Ví dụ 1
My father drives more carefully than my brother.
⟶
My brother doesn't drive as carefully as my father.
Đảo chủ ngữ + more...than → doesn't V as...as
Ví dụ 2
Jane cooks better than Mary.
⟶
Mary doesn't cook as well as Jane.
better → as well as (trạng từ gốc "well")
Công thức 2 — So sánh nhất ↔ Never
This is the most + adj + N + S + have ever V3
↔ S + have never V3 + a more + adj + N + than this
Ví dụ
This is the most interesting book I have ever read.
⟶
I have never read a more interesting book than this.
the most → a more · ever → never · thêm "than this"
Công thức 3 — No one ... than ↔ The ...-est
No one ... + adj-er + than A
↔ A + is + the + adj-est ...
Ví dụ
No one in our class is taller than Nam.
⟶
Nam is the tallest in our class.
No one ... taller than → the tallest
Công thức 4 — Không bằng ↔ So sánh hơn (đảo)
A is not as + adj + as B
↔ B is adj-er than A  ·  A is adj ngược-er than B
Ví dụ 1
A train is not as fast as a plane.
⟶
A plane is faster than a train.
Đảo chủ ngữ + not as fast as → faster than
Ví dụ 2
My house is bigger than your house.
⟶
Your house is smaller than my house.
Đảo chủ ngữ + đảo tính từ: bigger → smaller
Luôn nhớ đảo vị trí A và B khi chuyển đổi. Trạng từ: better → as well as, more carefully → as carefully as.

IV Câu bị động (Passive Voice)

Công thức chung
Chủ động: S + V + O
→ Bị động: O + be + V3 (+ by S)
ThìDạng bị động
Hiện tại đơn (speak/speaks)→am/is/are + V3
Quá khứ đơn (built)→was/were + V3
Hiện tại hoàn thành (has stolen)→has/have been + V3
Tương lai đơn (will finish)→will be + V3
Ví dụ 1 — Hiện tại đơn
People speak English all over the world.
⟶
English is spoken all over the world.
speak → is spoken · bỏ "by people" (chủ ngữ chung chung)
Ví dụ 2 — Quá khứ đơn
Alexander Graham Bell invented the telephone in 1876.
⟶
The telephone was invented in 1876 by Alexander Graham Bell.
invented → was invented · giữ "by + tên riêng"
Ví dụ 3 — Hiện tại hoàn thành
Someone has stolen my bike.
⟶
My bike has been stolen.
has stolen → has been stolen · bỏ "by someone"
Bỏ "by people / someone / they" khi chủ ngữ không quan trọng. Giữ "by + tên riêng" (by Alexander Graham Bell).

V Các cấu trúc khác (Other Structures)

1. It takes → Spend
It takes + O + time + to V
→ S + spend(s) + time + V-ing
It takes me 30 minutes to walk to school every day.
⟶
I spend 30 minutes walking to school every day.
2. Because → Because of
Because + S + V (mệnh đề)
→ Because of + N / V-ing (cụm danh từ)
Because it rained heavily, we stayed at home.
⟶
Because of the heavy rain, we stayed at home.
"rained heavily" (V + adv) → "the heavy rain" (danh từ)
3. Although → In spite of / Despite
Although + S + V (mệnh đề)
→ In spite of / Despite + N / V-ing
Although he was tired, he finished his homework.
⟶
In spite of being tired, he finished his homework.
"was tired" → "being tired" (V-ing) hoặc "his tiredness" (danh từ)
4. Let's → Suggested
"Let's + V ..." → S + suggested + V-ing
"Let's go to the cinema tonight," said Peter.
⟶
Peter suggested going to the cinema that night.
Let's go → suggested going · tonight → that night
5. Started ... ago → Have V3 ... for
S + started + V-ing + time + ago
→ S + have/has + V3 + for + time
I started learning English 3 years ago.
⟶
I have learned English for 3 years.
started V-ing ... ago → have V3 ... for
6. It's a pity → Wish
It's a pity + S + can't / don't + V
→ S + wish + S + could / Ved
It's a pity I can't speak English fluently.
⟶
I wish I could speak English fluently.
It's a pity → wish · can't → could (lùi thì)
Because/Although + mệnh đề (S+V). Because of / In spite of + cụm danh từ hoặc V-ing.
